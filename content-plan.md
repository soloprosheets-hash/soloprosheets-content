# SoloPro Sheets content plan

Read by the content agent every run. One topic per day, never repeated within 30 days.
The weekly analyst may reorder topics and add new ones at the bottom; it never deletes rows that have been posted.

## Voice

- Talk to a solo lash or brow artist running her own chair. Plain, warm, practical.
- One useful tip per post. The product is the last line, never the headline.
- Never promise income, never invent client results or testimonials, never claim to be a lash artist.
- Pins: title under 100 characters with the keyword near the start; description 2–3 sentences.
- Carousels: 5 slides. Slide 1 hook, slides 2–4 the tip, slide 5 "Free price calculator / full tracker, link in bio".

## Pillars

- **PRICE** – pricing services, raising prices, what to charge
- **MONEY** – profit, expenses, tax set-aside, slow months
- **CLIENTS** – rebooking, refills, retention, no-shows
- **SYSTEM** – tracking, spreadsheets, admin in 10 minutes a week
- **PRODUCT** – direct showcase of a tracker feature
